\# Changelog



\## Version 1.6

\- Renamed student id field from id to studentId.



\## Version 1.5

\- Increased student phone number field capacity from 20 to 25 characters.



\## Version 1.4

\- Increased student address field capacity from 100 to 200 characters.



\## Version 1.3

\- Increased student name field capacity from 50 to 100 characters.



\## Version 1.2

\- Added student gender field with a capacity of 10 characters.

\- Added semester information to the Student structure.

\- Added credit hours information to the Student structure.

\- Added student status field with a capacity of 20 characters.

\- Status can represent values such as Active, Inactive, or Graduated.



\## Version 1.1

\- Added email field to Student structure.

\- Added phone number field to Student structure.

\- Added student address field with a capacity of 100 characters.



\## Version 1.0

\- Added age field to Student structure.

\- Added CGPA field to Student structure.

\- Added department field to Student structure.
